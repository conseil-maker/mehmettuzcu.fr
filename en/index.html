Flagship project

Reflektif

AI-powered EdTech career guidance platform, France + Turkey.

An AI-augmented career guidance SaaS platform, natively bilingual French/Turkish, originally designed and coded solo and now developed with Mikail Lekesiz — president of our Turkish sister company Reflektif Bilişim (Istanbul), product & technology partner —, in active production since February 2026 and hosted in Europe (Frankfurt). I own its product design, technical architecture and pedagogical direction. The engine combines psychometrics (3 tests, 110 questions: RIASEC, Big Five, Values) and generative AI; the proprietary SmartMatch algorithm matches the user's profile against a catalogue of 928 occupations. A complete journey in production: tests, AI debriefs, document analysis, diagnostic report, occupation exploration, counselor portal with human validation and real-time messaging — with a B2B white-label cohort-management module.

Verified benchmarks: ≈167 000 lines of TypeScript/TSX, 71 PostgreSQL tables all protected by Row-Level Security, GDPR/KVKK compliance instrumented in the code.

Method

From business need to a compliant, automated tool

I don't paste a solution onto a problem. I follow a step-by-step method, structured and reproducible, that turns a field need into a compliant, automated system.

1

Clarify the problem

Understand the operational reality before anything else — field diagnosis, listening to the business teams.

2

Frame the legal side

Identify the applicable regulatory requirements from the start, to build compliance into the architecture.

3

Model the organization

Translate the need into a strategic architecture: roles, flows, data, control points.

4

Build the tooling

Design and develop the tool — full-stack or no-code/low-code depending on context, with a fast and defensible deployment.

5

Secure compliance

Instrument traceability, auditability and data protection within the system itself.

6

Automate

Industrialize recurring processes, with automations that are defensible, auditable and useful.

I decide with data: I model scenarios (optimistic, realistic, pessimistic) before acting decisively. And I favor objectivity and honest feedback over flattery.
